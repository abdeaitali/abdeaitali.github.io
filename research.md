## Academic Profile

I combine transport economics (socio-economic evaluation, cost-benefit analysis, life-cycle costing) with mathematical optimization for capacity allocation, timetabling, maintenance planning and passenger information in public transport and railways.

I hold an engineering diploma in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics from Grenoble INP ([Ensimag](https://ensimag.grenoble-inp.fr/)), an M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from [KTH – Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/), and a PhD in [Infra-Informatics](https://liu.se/en/article/forskarutbildning-i-infrainformatik) from [Linköping University](https://liu.se/en/about-liu).

I'm also active in linguistics, photography, and open-source (notably [Wikimedia](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Abdeaitali)).
