I teach and supervise students in transport economics, public transport, railway systems, optimization, and applied data analysis. My teaching connects analytical methods to real-world decisions in transportation systems.


## Examiner and main instructor
---
**[TNK127 – Optimization and Statistics in Transportation](https://studieinfo.liu.se/en/kurs/TNK127/ht-2023)** — Linköping University, Master's (English) (2023–present)

Introduces linear, nonlinear, and integer optimization together with probability and statistical analysis, applied to transportation problems. Uses data-driven project assignments and modelling exercises.

**[TNK113 – Transportekonomi](https://studieinfo.liu.se/en/kurs/TNK113/vt-2025)** — Linköping University, Master's (Swedish) (2025–present)

Transport economics course covering fundamental economic principles applied to transportation systems and infrastructure investment analysis.
